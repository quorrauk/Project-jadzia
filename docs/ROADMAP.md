| # | Project | Electronics | Python | Maths | Status |
|---|---------|-------------|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Ignition | Complete circuit, battery, wire, bulb, switch; current direction | `print()`, running code | Units: volts, amps, ohms | planned |
| 2 | Headlights | LED plus resistor; why LEDs burn out | First variables: `voltage = 9` | V = IR, substitution | planned |
| 3 | Dimmer | Variable resistor; dim the cockpit/eyes | Variables with arithmetic; calculate a resistor value | Rearranging formulas | planned |
| 4 | Switch Panel | Series vs parallel; backup lights | Text vs number variables; f-strings | Ratios, adding fractions | planned |
| 5 | Power Budget | P = IV; energy; battery life | Script that totals the whole robot's power use | Units, standard form | planned |
| 6 | Boss: Mission Day Failure | Diagnose with meters, fix the broken circuit | Short debug-the-script task | Mixed review | planned |
